2. Ultimate Engine Tweaks
https://www.nexusmods.com/stellarblade/mods/17

Open the game at least once and close it, then download Engine.ini to paste/replace in:
%localappdata%\SB\Saved\Config\WindowsNoEditor
then Right-Click on it -> Properties -> make sure the "Read-Only" box is selected -> Apply/OK
DO NOT USE TOGETHER WITH OTHERS "OPTIMIZATION" MODS (any other mod will be compatible)
